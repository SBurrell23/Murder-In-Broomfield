      How The Case Works

      The Table

      Everyone but the Forensic Scientist is dealt four Means of Murder and four pieces of Key Evidence, all face up. One of those players is the Murderer, and the weapon and the evidence they used are already sitting in plain sight.

      The Roles

      • Forensic Scientist — knows everything, and may not speak. Communicates only by placing bullet markers on the scene tiles.
      • Murderer — picks one weapon and one piece of evidence from their own hand, then talks their way out of it.
      • Investigators — read the tiles, argue, and spend their badge when they think they have it.
      • Accomplice — knows the murderer and exactly what they used. Wins with the murderer.
      • Witness — saw two figures in the dark but cannot tell which was which. Wins with the investigators.

      The Rounds

      The Scientist places one bullet on the Cause of Death tile, one on the Location of Crime tile, and one on each of four Scene Tiles. Then the table talks.

      The Cause of Death tile is the same every game — Suffocation, Severe Injury, Blood Loss, Illness/Disease, Poisoning, Accident — so the table learns to read it at a glance.

      At the end of a round the Scientist swaps one scene tile for a fresh one and marks it. This happens twice, giving three rounds in all.

      The Clock

      The host can set a round timer in the lobby, ten minutes by default. It starts once the opening bullets are down and restarts with each new round. Reaching zero changes nothing at all — it simply beeps, to tell the table it is time to move on.

      The Badge

      Every player except the Scientist carries one badge and may throw it at any moment — the murderer included, which is often the point. A badge names a suspect, a weapon and a piece of evidence. All three must be right. The Scientist rules on it, and a spent badge is gone for good.

      Winning

      The investigators win the moment a badge lands on all three. If every badge is spent or three rounds pass, the murderer walks.

      When a Witness is in play and a badge does land, the murderer’s side gets one last swing: name the witness correctly and they take the case anyway.

      Talking

      This game is played out loud. Keep your voice chat open — the app only tracks the evidence.

      Throw Your Badge

      Name the suspect, the weapon, and the evidence. All three must be right, and you only get one.
